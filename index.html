Position

Position is decided by your top language: whichever language GitHub reports on the most of your repos. One language per repo, so a huge project counts the the same as a tiny one. Ties go to whichever the API listed first.

Ratings

Every attribute is a log-scaled count compared against the top of the range, so the 10,000th star adds far less than the 10th. Nothing is clamped until the very end, which is why 99 is rare and actually means something.
